# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone. Participants are expected to communicate professionally, assume good intent, provide useful feedback, and respect different backgrounds and experiences.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and any conduct that would make a reasonable participant feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report concerns privately through the security or maintainer contact options described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential as far as practical.
